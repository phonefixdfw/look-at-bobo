# Look at Bobo: Vision

## The game in one line
A first-person game where you walk through a modern apartment, catch Bobo the cat doing something adorable, and shout "LOOK AT BOBO!"

## Who Bobo is
Bobo is a real cat, about 15 lb, and mostly black. He has white feet, a white bib, a white muzzle and a white vertical dash on his forehead. His eyes are yellow-green. In the game he is the star and never a villain or a prop.

## How it plays
1. You walk through a modern one-bedroom apartment in first person (mouse and keyboard).
2. Bobo is somewhere in the apartment doing something cute.
3. You find him, aim at him and press a button. Your hand points and a voice yells "LOOK AT BOBO!"
4. Fireworks and a celebration go off, and you get one point.
5. You respawn somewhere else in the apartment while Bobo moves on to another cute thing, and you search again.
6. At 10 points it rains Bobos all over the screen and the game says "your did it! you wind" (spelled exactly that way, on purpose).

## The ten moments (draft, to confirm)
Loafing on the couch · stretching on the bed · staring at a bird from the balcony window · head in a cardboard box · sitting in the sink · batting a pen off the desk · sleeping in a laundry basket · chasing his tail in the hallway · making biscuits on a blanket · sitting on the keyboard. *(These are first ideas. Real Bobo habits will replace some of them.)*

## The look
A realistic modern apartment, with believable lighting, materials and furniture. Bobo is a softly stylized, fur-shaded cat built from reference photos, so he reads clearly as Bobo without trying to be a photograph. The whole game is built in code, with no downloaded models or scans.

## The feel
Joyful and silly, and never scary or stressful. No timers or failure. Finding Bobo is the reward, and the fireworks make every catch feel like a win.

## Platform
A desktop web browser, playable from one shareable link. Keyboard and mouse. Phones are not a goal.

## Success looks like
Someone plays through all 10 points, laughs at the ending, and wants to show a friend.

## Principles
- Bobo is always cute and never in danger.
- The player always knows what to do: find Bobo, point, and shout.
- Always celebrated: every catch is a party.
- Nothing is invented about the real Bobo. His looks and habits come from his owner.
- Keep it short, about 10 to 15 minutes from start to finish.
*(Length is my guess. Tell me if you want it longer or shorter.)*

## Not goals (for now)
- Phones, touch controls and microphone shouting.
- Enemies, health, timers or failure states.
- Multiplayer.

## How we'll build it
The skill's ten steps, with a check-in after each:
1. Story, characters and locations.
2. A visual debugging tool.
3. The art style.
4. Voices, including the "LOOK AT BOBO!" yell. This is the only step that costs money, and only after you approve it.
5. A talking-character demo.
6. A playable slice of about five minutes.
7. Your playtest.
8. The rest of the game.
9. Polish.
10. Deploy, on the host you choose.

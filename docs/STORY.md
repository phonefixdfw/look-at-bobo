# Look at Bobo — Story (Step 1)

> Step 1 output only (story/characters/locations locked at the level the vision allows). No code, art, audio, or later-step work is included. Brandon approved `VISION.md` at repo root.

## Premise
A first-person, joyful, silly game where you walk through a modern one-bedroom apartment, find Bobo the cat doing something adorable, and shout "LOOK AT BOBO!" Every catch is a celebration. There are no timers, no failure states, and Bobo is never in danger.

## Core loop
1. The player spawns in the apartment in first person (mouse and keyboard).
2. Bobo is somewhere in the apartment doing one cute moment.
3. The player finds Bobo, aims at him, and presses the interact/shout button.
4. The player's hand points and a voice yells **"LOOK AT BOBO!"**
5. Fireworks and a celebration play, and the player earns **+1 point**.
6. The player respawns elsewhere in the apartment while Bobo moves to a different cute moment, and the search begins again.
7. At **10 points**, Bobos rain all over the screen and the game displays the ending text exactly as: `your did it! you wind`

## Structure and pacing
- **Length target (vision guess, to confirm):** ~10–15 minutes total from start to finish.
- **Progression:** 10 catches = 10 moments = 10 points. Each catch advances to the next moment/point.
- **Difficulty:** None by design. The challenge is playful searching, not pressure. The player always knows what to do: find Bobo, point, and shout.
- **Tone:** Joyful and silly, never scary or stressful. Finding Bobo is the reward.

## Story rules / truths (constraints for later steps)
- Bobo is the star and is always cute, never a villain or a prop.
- Bobo is never in danger and the player can never fail.
- The apartment is a believable, realistic modern space (art direction in later steps), but the **actual layout, furniture, and Bobo's real habits** come from Brandon.
